Ashby notes (jobs.ashbyhq.com/.../application):
- There are two file fields: "Autofill from resume" (skip it) and "Resume" (use upload_resume on this one).
- Yes/No questions are pairs of buttons; they appear as "(one of) [Yes | No]" fields: fill them with "Yes" or "No".
- Location is a combobox: give a city as written in answers.md; if it doesn't match, retry with one of the options listed.
- Acknowledgement checkboxes (arbitration agreement, "I confirm I have read the above") are legal statements: tick them only if answers.md has a line for them, otherwise log needs_info.
