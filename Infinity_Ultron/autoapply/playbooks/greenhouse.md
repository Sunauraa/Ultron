Greenhouse notes (job-boards.greenhouse.io):
- The form is on the job page itself, below the description.
- Most choice questions are comboboxes: fill them with the answer's text; if it doesn't match, the reply lists the options shown, so retry with one of them.

- Agreements (arbitration, AI policy, privacy) are legal statements: answer only if answers.md has a line for them, otherwise log needs_info.
